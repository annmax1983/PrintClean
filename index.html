Print the page, not the page furniture.

Reader extractionLocal typesettingA4 and Letter

PrintClean reads the page you are on, keeps the article, and drops the rest: menus, sidebars, ad slots, cookie banners, share bars, comment threads, newsletter boxes. What is left is typeset on a paper-like sheet in its own tab, where you set the font size, the line height and the measure, choose A4 or Letter, pick a margin, and then print it or save it as a PDF with the browser’s own dialog. Nothing is uploaded and no page is refetched from a server. Batch mode turns a list of addresses into one clean sheet per article.
